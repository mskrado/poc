Regional freight costs rose 4.2% quarter over quarter, driven by fuel surcharges in the western corridor and a shortage of drayage capacity near the two largest ports.

Contracted lanes absorbed most of the increase. Spot-market exposure remained below the 18% internal ceiling for the fourth consecutive quarter.
